Current Research

  • Source-Aware Dynamic Pricing with Strategic Buyers
    July 2024 – Present

    We study personalized pricing with high-dimensional customer features from different sources: some features are verified, whereas others can be strategically misreported by buyers seeking lower prices. The seller learns from binary purchase feedback without knowing the market noise distribution.

    We develop Source-Aware Localized Exploration and Calibrated Exploitation (SALE), which uses verified features and past observations to construct personalized exploration intervals and calibrates exploitation prices for strategic manipulation. Our analysis establishes sublinear regret guarantees and quantifies the value of verified information in reducing exploration costs.
